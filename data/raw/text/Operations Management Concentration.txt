Operations Management Concentration
The Operations Management concentration equips students with the concepts and tools associated with the key
levers for designing and managing an organization’s business processes. The concentration allows students to dive
deeper into the fundamental principles underlying state‐of‐the‐art operations management practices, such as
business analytics, service operations, supply chain management, inventory and manufacturing, retailing, and lean
operations. The Operations Management concentration prepares students for career roles in consulting,
entrepreneurship, supply chain and general management.
