Decision Sciences Concentration
This concentration provides students with an opportunity to acquire in-depth knowledge of the business analytics
tools and applications. The concentration equips students with a skill set that is increasingly important in many
managerial roles and industries: how to structure and analyze information and data to improve business decisions,
processes and strategies. The concentration requires students to take a total of 18 credits, with at least 6 credits in
foundational business analytics courses (the first category below), and at least 9 credits from the second category,
which consider applications of business analytics approach and tools across other functional areas. The final 3 credits
can come from either category. The first category empowers students with in-depth business analytics tools, while
the second category provides students with understanding of breadth of applications of these tools. For that reason,
it is strongly suggested that the courses from the second category involve at least three different area designations
(ACCOUNTG, DECISION, FINANCE, FUQINTRD, MARKETNG, MGRECON, OPERATNS, STRATEGY).
