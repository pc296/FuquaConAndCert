HSM  Certificate
Certificate    Requirements
A certificate in Health Sector Management (HSM) requires the completion of all Fuqua Daytime MBA core
course requirements, three required HSM courses: HLTHMGMT 710 and a Seminar Series in two parts
(HLTHMGMT 705 & 706). Additionally, must complete at least one Industry Context Elective and at least one
additional course with a HLTHMGMT number. Must take four courses in total from the elective list.
Additional  Concentrations
The HSM curriculum is flexible, allowing students to combine their interest in health care with one additional
concentration, certificate, or MSTeM second major within the Daytime and Accelerated MBA program.
Concurrently, HSM cultivates collaborative learning opportunities with other noted Duke programs, including
engineering, basic science, public policy, law, global health and medicine, as well as with leading health care
companies and organizations across the globe.
Enrollment
Any current Daytime MBA student may participate in the HSM certificate program; please send your current
resume and a brief statement of interest, including your goals within the health care industry, to the HSM office.
