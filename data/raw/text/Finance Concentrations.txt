Finance Concentrations
Three Options:
There are 3 finance concentration options: Concentration in Finance (Corporate), Concentration in Finance
(Investment) or a Dual Finance Concentration. To receive 1 of these concentrations, a student must take at least 6
electives that are beyond the required core courses, and are from the set of electives specified for each
concentration.
Option    1: Concentration       in Finance     (Corporate)
This concentration is designed for the following career paths: Corporate Finance, Investment Banking, Venture
Capital and Private Equity. Students should note that electives may have pre-requisite requirements. In addition, not
all finance electives are offered each year, and most electives are offered once per academic year.
2 Required Courses:
FINANCE 646 — Corporate Finance
FINANCE 647 — Investment
Choose 1 of the following courses:
ACCOUNTG 592 — Detecting Earnings Management
ACCOUNTG 597 — Financial Statement Analysis
ACCOUNTG 598 — Valuation & Fundamental Analysis
Choose 1 of the following courses:
DECISION 611 — Decision Models
FUQINTRD 697 — Innovation and Cryptoventures
Choose 2 courses from the following set of finance electives:
FINANCE 651 — Entrepreneurial Finance
FINANCE 652 — Project Finance
FINANCE 654 — Advanced Corporate Finance
FINANCE 658 — Corporate Restructuring
FINANCE 660 — Private Capital Markets
FINANCE 661 — Raising Capital and Financial Technologies
Option    2: Concentration       in Finance     (Investment)
This concentration is designed for the following career paths: Asset Management, Private Wealth Management, and
Research, Sales & Trading. Students should note that electives may have pre-requisite requirements. In addition, not
all finance electives are offered each year, and most electives are offered once per academic year.
Both of the following courses are required:
FINANCE 646 — Corporate Finance
FINANCE 647 — Investment
Choose 1 of the following courses:
ACCOUNTG 597 — Financial Statement Analysis
ACCOUNTG 598 — Valuation & Fundamental Analysis
Choose 1 of the following courses:
DECISION 611 — Decision Models
DECISION 614 — Forecasting
FUQINTRD 697 — Innovation and Cryptoventures
MGRECON 781 — The World Economy
Choose 2 courses from the following set of finance electives:
FINANCE 648 — Derivatives
FINANCE 653 — Fixed Income Securities and Risk Management
FINANCE 656 — Global Asset Allocation & Stock Selection
FINANCE 663 — International Finance
Option    3: Dual   Finance    Concentration        (Corporate     and
Investment)
The dual concentration counts as 2 concentrations. Two concentrations are the maximum
number of academic specialties one can earn.
Students wishing to obtain a dual concentration in Finance (corporate and investment) are required to complete a
total of 10 courses which are broken down below. Students should note that electives may have pre-requisite
requirements. In addition, not all finance electives are offered each year, and most electives are offered once per year.
2 required courses:
FINANCE 646 — Corporate Finance
FINANCE 647 — Investment
Choose 1 of the following courses:
ACCOUNTG 592 — Detecting Earnings Management
ACCOUNTG 597 — Financial Statement Analysis
ACCOUNTG 598 — Valuation & Fundamental Analysis
Choose 1 of the following courses:
DECISION 611 — Decision Models
DECISION 614 — Forecasting
FUQINTRD 697 — Innovation and Cryptoventures
MGRECON 781 — The World Economy
Choose 2 from the following set of corporate electives:
FINANCE 651 — Entrepreneurial Finance
FINANCE 652 — Project Finance
FINANCE 654 — Advance Corporate Finance
FINANCE 658 — Corporate Restructuring
FINANCE 660 — Private Capital Markets
FINANCE 661 — Raising Capital and Financial Technologies
Choose 2 from of the following set of investment electives:
FINANCE 648 — Derivatives
FINANCE 653 — Fixed Income Securities and Risk Management
FINANCE 656 — Global Asset Allocation & Stock Selection
FINANCE 663 — International Finance
Choose 2 additional courses from either the corporate elective set or the investment elective set.
Finance    Certificate
A certificate in finance is also available. Students wishing to obtain the Certificate of Academic Excellence in Finance
are required to complete a total of 10 courses. Learn more about requirements for the Finance Certificate.
